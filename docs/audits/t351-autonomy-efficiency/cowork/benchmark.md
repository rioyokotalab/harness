## Evidence and iteration method

Record exact line/word counts; GitHub run/event/job counts and rounded-minute
proxies; official documentation URLs; `/usr/bin/time` wall/user/system/RSS;
selected test names and reasons; tree/manifest/environment digests; lock and
temporary-root identities; exit status; bounded output; and any unknown.

Thresholds:

- at least 85% lower median mandatory words, with at least 50% lower words in
  every scenario;
- at least 30% lower median non-ledger policy/skill words, so archive movement
  alone cannot satisfy skill-routing acceptance;
- active board at most 250 lines and 2,500 words with indexed tracked history;
- docs-only Local validation at most five seconds and skill-only at most ten
  seconds, using matched same-host `/usr/bin/time` samples;
- exact mapped tests for narrow code, complete escalation for broad/unknown
  and validator/safety/lifecycle changes; the workflow, full runner, focused
  manifest, cleanup helper, impact map, receipt code, and router themselves
  always escalate;
- exact-tree receipt reuse only under an unchanged validation contract;
- at least 90% lower estimated private hosted minutes for ordinary owner work;
- no hosted runner for a trusted owner job when its required job can be
  conditionally skipped, but unchanged hosted execution for untrusted work;
- one scheduled weekly full hosted opportunity in each private repository,
  plus manual dispatch, provides independent post-hoc drift detection without
  claiming a hard completion bound from a best-effort scheduler;
- three concurrent project validations with no cross-project mutation or
  lifecycle lock;
- complete unchanged suite passing once before publication.

For every candidate: state a falsifiable hypothesis, record before digest/diff,
run the named case on exact inputs, record result and safety evidence, then
write `keep` or `revert` with reason.  Unchanged retries are not iterations.
Facts outrank inference; matched measurements outrank prose.

## Stop condition

Stop a candidate on an ambiguous target/head/tree/lock/result, missing external
authority, protected digest drift, credential or pane exposure, live-session
impact, untrusted persistent-self-host execution, unsafe cleanup boundary, or
failed critical safety fixture.  Stop new work at 2026-07-31 09:00 JST and
perform durable reconciliation, cleanup, and fleet health.
