# Objective

Make producer and consumer roles coordination defaults rather than different
repository permission classes across Harness, Personal, Students, Swallow,
and Website.

# Acceptance

Consumers normally defer queue curation to the producer, but may update
producer paths or allocate a durable task when they judge that necessary to
solve an in-scope issue. Role-aware diff checks warn rather than reject solely
because of role; immutable packets and repository safety invariants remain
hard gates. Plain-language contextual owner approval is sufficient without
copying agent-authored authorization text. Apply equivalent project-local
policy and regressions independently in all five repositories, preserve their
privacy and domain boundaries, validate fully, and publish each through its
protected workflow.
