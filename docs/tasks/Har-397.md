# Har-397 — Separate nightly work from ordinary Harness tasks

## Frozen decision

Harness executes its own work directly and is not its own producer/consumer
pair. Ordinary work remains in the `Har-*` ledger rooted at `TODO.md` and
`docs/tasks/`. Owner requests explicitly naming the nightly ledger instead use
the independent `Nit-*` ledger rooted at `NIGHTLY.md` and `docs/nightly/`.

Harness remains the portfolio coordinator for repository-local producer and
consumer ledgers in Personal, Students, Swallow, and Website. Their files,
policies, permissions, privacy boundaries, and histories are out of scope.

## Implementation

1. Add a validated direct-ledger contract for `Har-*` and `Nit-*`, including
   separate namespaces, next IDs, compact active boards, and task records.
2. Move the superseded Harness producer packets and consumer receipts into a
   dated historical archive, preserving provenance without cold-start reads.
3. Remove Harness-only producer selector, assignment, checkpoint parser,
   proportional consumer validator, role wrapper, tests, and command surface.
4. Keep the reusable external-project onboarding scaffold and portfolio policy,
   but make their non-Harness scope explicit.
5. Update README, routing, context benchmarks, focused suites, and complete
   validation before protected publication.

## Outcome

- `TODO.md` and `docs/tasks/` are the direct `Har-*` ledger; `NIGHTLY.md` and
  `docs/nightly/` are the independent `Nit-*` ledger.
- A nightly-ledger request allocates `Nit-*`; ordinary work remains `Har-*`.
- The former Harness packet, assignment, receipt, selector, checkpoint,
  proportional-consumer, role-wrapper, and productive-idle runtime are retired.
  Historical packets and receipts remain under the dated history archive.
- Root cold-start, README, validation routing, and focused tests describe and
  enforce the direct model. The external-project onboarding scaffold remains
  available and explicitly excludes Harness.

## Validation

Focused synthetic Har/Nit tests, direct task-ledger routing, policy routing,
validation routing, Claude takeover, onboarding scaffold validation, context
benchmark refresh, diff checks, public audit, and the final complete phase-one
gate are required on the publication candidate.

## Current next action

Publish the validated candidate through protected main, verify exact readback,
and remove the isolated worktree through guarded housekeeping.
