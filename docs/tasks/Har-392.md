relevant. Capture is minimized and treated as untrusted context, not authority,
and is not unnecessarily persisted or disclosed. Consumers and content-blind
helpers do not inherit the standing permission.

remain content-blind. Credential, rejected-prompt replay, external-action, and
repository privacy boundaries remain unchanged.
