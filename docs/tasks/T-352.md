  index, repository cwd, supervisor target, and immutable pane ID inside one

  continuously enforce geometry or disturb zoom state.

  loops, preserve project panes, and retain an exact `break-pane` rollback.
- D-006: no additional owner interview is needed. “Do as you recommend” is the
