# Har-400 — AL session renewal restart defect

T-302's AL keepalive holds a persistent multiplexed master so the one-day
CSCS certificate boundary does not interrupt service, restarts every 60
seconds on transport loss, and halts permanently on authentication failure
(`RestartPreventExitStatus=77`) because automating MFA renewal is out of
scope. The 2026-08-05 21:14 JST CSCS outage dropped the master; both
restarts hit the expired certificate and the unit correctly froze in
`renewal-required`.

The defect was in the recovery path: after the owner renewed, `harness
al-session --start` refused with the *recorded* `renewal-required` without
probing whether authentication now works, and the only way forward was an
undocumented manual `--stop`. Reproduced live on 2026-08-06: a fresh
non-multiplexed probe exited 0 while `--start` still refused.

## Acceptance

- An explicit `--start` over a receipt-matched halted marked unit probes
  fresh authentication first.
- On probe success it retires the halted generation, its receipt, and any
  receipt-stale socket, then starts and receipts a new marked generation.
- On probe failure it reports the fresh classification with
  `ownership=managed`, leaves the unit, receipt, and socket state
  untouched, and status still reports the halted classification.
- Recovering, external, stale, and no-receipt start paths are unchanged; no
  credential is read, renewed, or automated; MFA remains owner-only.

## Outcome

`--start` now treats a receipt-matched halted unit as the designed
post-renewal recovery: the residual-unit check is skipped for that path,
the existing fresh probe runs first, and only a working route discards the
failed generation before a new marked unit is created and receipted. A
failing probe reports its fresh classification truthfully as
`ownership=managed` and preserves all evidence.

The owning suite's renewal fixture now covers still-failing-auth refusal
with evidence preservation, post-renewal recovery with a fresh marker, and
ready/managed status afterwards; ShellCheck passes at warning level. The
complete tier-R3 phase-one suite passed on the final integrated tree before
publication. Live recovery on Local after the owner's 2026-08-06 renewal:
one `--start` replaced the halted generation, status reports the target
ready and managed, and multiplexed `ssh al true` passes.

Owner recovery contract after a future authentication halt: renew the CSCS
certificate, then run `harness al-session --start` once.
