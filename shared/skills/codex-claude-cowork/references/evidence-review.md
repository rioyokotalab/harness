# Cowork evidence review

Read completely before evidence design, production, critique, reconciliation,
or `ready-for-execution`.

## Independent and reciprocal evidence

Use separate writable sandboxes from one immutable baseline. Network, packages,
schedulers, remote writes, or messages require independent authorization from
the frozen task and no adequate local experiment.

Give each client charter, plan, baseline, benchmark, and acceptance gates;
withhold conclusions until both independent passes finish. Evidence identifies
sandbox and baseline, records commands or tool actions with bounded results,
separates observation from inference, criticizes concrete claims, and proposes
exact changes. A prose-only review is insufficient when a safe experiment is
feasible.

Freeze `driver-evidence.md`, seal protected driver state, and make no live
driver write during the co-pilot window. Reveal both files through a fresh
reciprocal stage and require complete evidence plus critique. Resolve the
strongest conflict with a matched test or record it unresolved.

## Reconcile

Prefer matched observations, then primary repository contracts, deterministic
static analysis, explicit inference, and unsupported opinion. Preserve minority
evidence. Empirically resolve disagreement affecting safety, scope, correctness,
irreversible state, or acceptance, or ask the owner before execution.

The frozen reconciliation records ordered target steps, sole target writer,
accepted and rejected changes, uncertainty, preconditions, rollback, stop
conditions, authority boundaries, per-step and final checks, owner go status,
and exact next action. Never execute by majority vote.
