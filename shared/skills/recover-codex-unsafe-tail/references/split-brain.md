# Same-ID split-brain classification

A matching thread ID is not synchronization evidence. Compare the TUI argv and
open-rollout identity with app-server `thread/read` plus its reciprocal socket.
A standalone `--last` or local-session TUI opening a rollout that the app server
serves, without that TUI being its remote peer, is split-brain.

Freeze new prompts and reconcile Git/ledger truth. `idle` never makes dual
ownership safe. Preserve both clients until the owner selects one writer.
Cross-surface use requires one exact remote-backed TUI. If its controller or
identity is unavailable, keep the selected client sole writer and use a
distinct thread on every other surface. Never replay either continuation.
